## [Unreleased]

### Added

- 🙂 **Face unlock in the notch.** Wake your Mac and look at it: the notch grows out of the camera housing, plays a
  Face ID–style scan, folds into a tick and types your password into the lock screen. A red shake when it isn't you;
  hover over the notch to try again. An island at the top of the screen on Macs without a notch.
- 🪞 **The notch's look**: shaped like the real housing, solid black at the top melting into clear Liquid Glass.
- 🔄 **Face ID–style setup**: move your head to complete a ring of ticks. Add more appearances any time.
- 🛡️ **Guardrails** like face unlock on iPhone: armed by Touch ID, and off again after 45 minutes locked (your choice),
  two days, or five missed scans in a row.
- 🔐 Your password in the keychain, checked when saved and in memory only while armed. Faces stored encrypted.
- ⚙️ Settings for when to look, the camera, match sensitivity, asking for a blink, and your appearances.
- ⬆️ Updates with Sparkle.
